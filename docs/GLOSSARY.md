# Glossary

The terms the code and these docs use, in alphabetical order. A word with several meanings has one row per meaning, qualified in brackets; [Preferred words](#preferred-words) names the word prose should use for each. Code identifiers keep their names: the recommendations apply to documentation, comments and commit messages.

| Capture (review)         | A screenshot taken for review by the capture recipe.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | [Scene modes](SCENE-MODES.md#capture-recipe), [Testing](TESTING.md#visual-review)                                    |

| Cel band                 | The grade's posterising step: luma rounded to fifths, mixed in at `celMix` (0.24). In film only the sky takes it, the clouds' painterly steps; the subjects, mountains, ground and the light shafts' air shade continuously.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | postprocess.js, quality.js `postprocessSettings.celMix`                                                              |
| Cloud field              | The film's cloud banks on the sky shell, as GLSL statements from the drift clock to the bank cover. The sky shell draws them, and the stars evaluate them where their view rays leave the shell, so they hide behind the banks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | [estate-sky.js](../src/scene/estate-sky.js) `cloudFieldGLSL`                                                         |
| Cloud reshaping          | The change that gives the sky outside the reference banks broad, swirling, layered banks like theirs. It works through two weights, the open weight on the bank density and the bend weight on the noise's coordinates, both exactly 0 over the reference banks. Through the bend weight come horizon decompression, an even enlargement of the noise (`CLOUD_RESHAPE.scale`) and a swirl driven by a half-frequency octave; through the open weight the same octave decides bank or clear sky, with darker lanes from the next octave inside the banks. The density lift leaves the roof's clear lane alone, and behind the name and intro the text guard takes density off the banks. Off in the environment capture. | estate-sky.js `CLOUD_RESHAPE`, `uCloudReshape`                                                                       |
| Commit point             | A moment when a change can land without a visible pop: before the reveal, inside the fade-in, on a tour cut under the kept frame, or on any frame while the tour is not running.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md#scene-lifecycle), terrain-build.js `unseen()`, runtime.js `createDeferredQualityStep` |

| Composition profile      | The camera preset and world offset chosen from the viewport (`compact`, `desktop`, `portraitPhone`, `landscapePhone`, `tabletPortrait`): it frames the orbit camera, sets the camera's base field of view on resize and lowers the environment root by `sceneOffsetY`. | Deep link                | `#about`, `#profile`, `#experience` or `#contact` (or a `-text` alias) opens that dialog through About; the URL follows the open dialog with `history.replaceState`, adding no history entries.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | [deep-links.js](../src/ui/deep-links.js)                                                                             |

It never passes through black.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | camera-tour.js `TOUR_TRANSITION`, postprocess.js `LAYER_STAGGER`                                                     |

| Dwell                    | How long the tour stays on a shot, cut to cut: 9, 7 or 6 s by shot, or a fixed 3, 5 or 20 s with `?tour=`. | Fallback (status)        | An asset, map or build that failed: `status: "fallback"`. A fallback tower or tree stops the scene for the title card; the lantern, slate maps, rocks and ranges keep what was there.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | [architecture-assets.js](../src/scene/architecture-assets.js), [Scene modes](SCENE-MODES.md#scenedebug)              |
| Far plain air            | What the band under the ranges, below eye level and past the film terrain's edge, reads as: distant dark air. That band is the ranges' lower bodies, which eases into the terrain's slate (`TERRAIN_HORIZON`) lifted 1.65× at eye level and rising to 1.9× where the view meets the terrain's edge (`TERRAIN_EDGE`); the mountain feet haze to the same air, and the slate's far edge darkens toward 1.75× the slate, never lightening. The ranges meet it through a rocky skirt (`HORIZON_SKIRT`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | [hill-silhouette.js](../src/scene/hill-silhouette.js) `HORIZON_AIR`, mud-ground.js                                   |

| Gobo                     | The light shafts' light where it lands on the subject: soft, broken patches on bark and timber; a shot's `shafts.gobo` can set its own (Portrait's silver edges) and take it off behind About (`about`, Masonry study and Gallery detail).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | light-shafts.js                                                                                                      |
| Governor                 | The adaptive quality controller: after the reveal it samples display intervals and steps the tier down under sustained load, and back up once the display keeps up. `?quality=high` or `balanced` pins it (no adaptive steps); `auto` leaves it free, and `low` never reaches the scene.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | quality.js `createSceneQualityGovernor`                                                                              |

| Grade                    | The colour pass on both tiers: contrast, cool shadows, warm highlights, the cel band and the ink contour (in film, the sky's alone).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | postprocess.js, quality.js `postprocessSettings`                                                                     |
| Grain (scene)            | The final vignette-grain pass's noise, fixed to a 1280×720 grid over the canvas and on both tiers (`postprocessGrain`); 0.008 strength in film. | Grain (title card)       | A faint anti-banding grain over the title card's night: a 150 px inline SVG tile of white and black specks that balance on the night. It fades with the canvas's reveal (a sibling selector, so the vignette must follow the scene host), is absent under forced colours and increased contrast, and stays on the 404.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | [styles.css](../styles.css) `.scene-vignette::before`                                                                |
| Hold (render)            | Rendering stops: the `"panel"` hold 450 ms after a dialog opens, and the `"visitor"` hold of a visitor pause. | Hold (sampling)          | The 3 s after a resize, resume, context restore, dialog release or asset commit during which the governor takes no samples.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | quality.js `SAMPLE_HOLD_MS`, `holdSampling()`                                                                        |
| Hold (shot)              | The code's name for a dwell.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | directed-shots.js `hold`                                                                                             |
| Hold (tour)              | The tour stops advancing on its current shot: an open dialog, reduced motion or a visitor pause. It resumes without a time jump.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | camera-tour.js                                                                                                       |
| Horizon band             | The film sky's faint lift at the horizon (`filmBand`); the ranges read it too, through `FILM_SKY_GLSL`, for the sky behind them. Not the far plain.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | estate-sky.js `FILM_SKY_GLSL`                                                                                        |
| Horizon decompression    | The cloud reshaping's first term: past a knee on the cloud plane the noise's radius grows more slowly than the plane's, so banks near the horizon keep about the reference banks' size instead of shrinking, with the plane's perspective, into small ovals.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | estate-sky.js `CLOUD_RESHAPE.horizon`                                                                                |
| Ink contour              | The grade's edge darkening from the luma difference between the samples one CSS pixel either side of each pixel, across and down (`uTexelSize` is one CSS pixel); in film only where a pixel and its four neighbours are all sky (the clouds' own edges).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | postprocess.js `uInkMix`, `uTexelSize`                                                                               |
| Kept frame               | The outgoing shot's graded frame, stored at the tour capture and dissolved into the live shot; it keeps pushing in by `zoom`. Changes that would pop land under it on a cut.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | postprocess.js, camera-tour.js                                                                                       |
| Knee                     | A soft highlight limit, `c / (1 + luma(c) / knee)`: a reflection or glint approaches a ceiling instead of clipping. The moon's glint uses 0.06, the lantern's 0.35, and reflections behind text 0.035, blended in across the text guard's reach (`slateTextKnee`): scaled into the divisor, a knee that low cuts a hard edge into bright glare.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | mud-ground.js `SLATE_WATER`                                                                                          |
| Lazy chunk               | A scene chunk loaded by dynamic `import()`: `terrain-build`, `mountain-build`, `rock-build`, `light-shafts` and `lantern-flame`. Outside the 820 KiB scene budget.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | build.mjs, [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md#module-map)                                                                |
| Lease                    | A hold on a decoded model's textures, geometries and images through staging, commit and restoration; aborted and stale results release through the same path, and the resources are freed with the last lease.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | architecture-assets.js `retain()`                                                                                    |
| Light shafts             | The lazy, WebGL2-only air light and gobo: warm star rays in The watch, cool moonbeams in Threshold, Masonry study, Gallery detail, Watch and tree, Portrait and Close-up, none in the two lantern shots.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | light-shafts.js                                                                                                      |

| Massifs                  | The owner's three Meshy mountains (ridge, spine, summit) set as copies around the camera; each placement is one eye in its model's frame inside the envelope its GLB was culled for.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | [mountain-build.js](../src/scene/mountain-build.js) `RANGE_PLACEMENTS`, hill-silhouette.js `MASSIFS`                 |
| Moon key                 | The scene's directional key light and its only shadow caster, placed at `directionalPosition` (32, 28, 14) in index.js's `lightingConfig`; the environment capture centres its moon glow on that direction (`keyDirection`), and mud-ground.js `MOON_LIGHTS.key` restates it (a test holds them equal). | Open weight              | `open`, the cloud reshaping's weight on the bank density: exactly 0 over the reference banks and the roof's clear lane, 1 in the open sky clear of them, scaled by `uCloudReshape` (1 for the sky and the stars, 0 in the environment capture). | Orbit camera             | The camera the hidden canvas keeps until a directed shot is ready.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | index.js `ORBIT_START_ANGLE`, `ORBIT_SPEED`                                                                          |
| Override tier            | `?quality=high`, `balanced` or `low`.
